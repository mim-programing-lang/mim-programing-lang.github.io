**2026-11-15.** **Meem 0.17.3** is out, together with **Meem editor 0.5.0** (which has Meem 0.17.3 built in), on the [download page](/en/download/). It is the beta before 1.0, and what we ask you to test is in [Try 0.17.3 with us](/en/beta/).

Between 0.10.0 and 0.17.3 there are seven releases that were not published before today, and some of them **break** 0.10 programs:

- **Sized numbers:** `عدد64` and `كسر64` instead of `عدد` and `كسر`, and conversion is explicit ([migrating to 0.12](/en/docs/migration/to-0-12/)).
- **The optional type:** `ن؟` is checked before use, and a string cannot be `لاشيء` (nothing) ([migrating to 0.11](/en/docs/migration/to-0-11/)).
- **Tuples, enums with a payload, generics and traits:** new lessons on [tuples](/en/learn/tuples/) and [generics and traits](/en/learn/generics/), and the reference in [generics](/en/docs/language/generics/).
- **The `مجموعات` (collections) library:** a stack, a queue and a set ([lesson 19](/en/learn/collections/)).
- **Memory:** temporaries are freed by themselves, the confirmed leak `E0472` is a compile error, and `--strict-memory`.
- **Input and output:** line-by-line reading, binary files and byte strings.
- **The warning policy:** thirteen warnings became compile errors, and what the compiler cannot prove is behind `--warn-unproven`.

And Meem editor 0.5.0: go to definition, rename symbol, completion of enum members and tuple fields, and highlighting of the 0.17 forms.

All the details are in [the changelog](/en/docs/changelog/). Every example on the site and in the reference handbook was run with the 0.17.3 archive when the site was built.
