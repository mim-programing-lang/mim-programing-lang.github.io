## Why support

Meem is a programming language with Arabic keywords and right-to-left code, and one person works on it. A donation helps keep the language and the website in development, and helps prepare the [playground](/en/playground/) to run programs in the browser.

<DonateButton />

## What a donation means

A donation is optional. It grants no privileges, no technical support, and no right to the code or the licence, and no product or service is given in return. The project is not a charity.

## How it works

The button opens the PayPal payment page (an online payment service) in a new tab, and the payment is made and processed there under PayPal's terms and privacy policy.

The button is a plain link: this page loads nothing from PayPal. The site collects no data about you, and does not know who clicked the button or donated. What PayPal shows the account holder receiving the donation is decided by PayPal and its settings.

## Other ways to help

- Share the site with anyone who cares about learning to program in Arabic.
- Try the language: start with [getting started](/en/start/), then the [lessons](/en/learn/).
- Report the problems you find, or suggest what is missing, in the [issues repository](https://github.com/mim-programing-lang/meem-feedback/issues). How to write a good report is in [{betaLabelEn}](/en/beta/).
