Nineteen lessons in three levels. Each lesson has a goal, an estimated time, prerequisites, a short explanation with working examples, an exercise with its solution folded away, and common mistakes linked to their IDs in the [error catalog](/en/docs/errors/catalog/), then the next lesson. Every example and every solution was run with Meem 0.10.0 when the site was built, and the output under it is what it actually printed. The code and Meem's keywords are Arabic, as they are in the language itself.

Tick «Done» on a lesson when you finish it to follow your progress.
