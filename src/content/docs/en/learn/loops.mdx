## Goal

To repeat with `لكل` (for), `مدى` (range) and `طالما` (while), and to stop a loop with `توقف` (break).

## Explanation

- A **loop** repeats a block of lines.
- `لكل ع في مدى(1, 11):` goes through the numbers from 1 up to, but not including, 11.
- `طالما شرط:` repeats as long as the condition is true.
- `توقف` (break) leaves the loop, and `تجاوز` (continue) moves to the next round.
- `مج += ع` is short for `مج = مج + ع`.
