## Goal

To choose between two paths with `اذا` (if) and `غير ذلك` (else).

## Explanation

- A **condition** is a question whose answer is `صواب` (true) or `خطاء` (false).
- An `اذا` line ends with a colon `:`. The lines indented under it form the **block**, which runs if the condition holds. Each indentation level is 4 spaces.
- `غير ذلك اذا` (else if) is an alternative condition, and `غير ذلك` covers everything left.
